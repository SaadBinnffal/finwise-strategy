# The Model · FinWise

> Module 6 · Proposed recommendation to FinWise leadership. No pricing test has run.

## Stage + model

Prioritize **Stage 1 · Value Creation** for the trial experience, despite $10M ARR and PMF. The funnel table shows **128 paid conversions from 6,424 trials (1.99%)**; reported modeling usage fell from **58.06% in September 2024 to 36.04% in October 2024**. These signals justify investigating first value; they do not establish a pricing cause.

Retain **Freemium with the existing reverse trial**, monetizing ongoing value through paid workspace subscriptions for recurring cash planning. Free access supports activation and collaboration but risks fewer upgrades and higher servicing costs; validate costs before expansion.

## Recommendation

Hold base prices and billing terms. For new trial workspaces, test one packaging boundary: keep own-data import, one editable 30-day cash forecast, and optional accountant review available without payment; charge for additional forecast scenarios. Preserve manual weekly review of the first forecast.

First audit existing entitlements, payment exposure, and unit costs. If this first-value path is already free, retain it and prioritize the M5 activation experiment instead of relabeling existing functionality as a pricing change.

**Pricing bet — packaging matched to value:** If a verified pre-value payment barrier is removed, more importers will reach a useful first forecast, while additional planning needs can sustain paid upgrades. Neither the barrier nor willingness to pay for extra scenarios is established.

## Model signal

Target **M4 Leading 2:** the proportion of successful own-data importers reaching a first valid model by trial day 7. The supplied Modeling (%) column does not measure this conditional rate.

Report this diagnostically alongside activation across all assigned workspaces. If entitlement changes occur only after trial expiry, measure post-expiry completion separately with equal follow-up; do not claim a day-7 effect.

Run a separate workspace-level comparison after the M5 test; keep invitation availability equal across variants. Assess **90-day revenue after discounts/refunds per originally assigned workspace**, paid conversion, and retained useful use. Guardrails cover servicing cost, onboarding abandonment, and retention deterioration. Predefine feasible thresholds and sample requirements before launch. Roll out only with credible commercial improvement and acceptable guardrails.
